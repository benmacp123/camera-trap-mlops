# Trust report: DINOv2 probe

Generated by `make trust-report` from committed predictions. A retrospective audit of sites with labels; it gives no accuracy for a new, unlabelled site. Fold sites are optimistic (the model was chosen on them); hold-out sites were scored once by the frozen model.

## Review budget

A sequence is accepted without review when the model's top probability is at least the threshold; the rest are reviewed and assumed corrected. Residual error is what is left wrong in the final data. The suggested threshold is the lowest whose residual error on fold sites is at most 5%, chosen on fold sites only.
